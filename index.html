About Our Home Kitchen

Ghar Ka Khana started the way most good things do: too much dal makhani after a Sunday family lunch, and neighbours asking for a dabba. We are a small, all-veg home kitchen that cooks North Indian food the way it is made at home, slow, patient and without shortcuts.

No preservatives, no taste makers from a packet, no mass production. Just fresh vegetables from the local sabzi mandi, ghee where it matters, masalas roasted and ground at home, and the same recipes that have run in the family for three generations.

  • Small batches, cooked fresh every morning
  • Completely vegetarian kitchen
  • Whole spices, no artificial flavouring
  • Packed in food-grade, microwave-safe boxes
